# 📊 Vrinda Store Annual Report Dashboard - Excel

## 🧾 Overview

This Excel-based dashboard offers a comprehensive view of Vrinda Store’s sales performance throughout 2024. It enables users to explore customer demographics, channel performance, sales distribution, and order trends—all through dynamic visuals and slicers.

---

## 📌 Key Highlights

- **Top Sales Amount**: ₹2.99M (Maharashtra)  
- **Top Sales Channel**: **Amazon (35%)**  
- **Gender-based Sales**: **Women (64%)**, **Men (36%)**  
- **Order Status**: **Delivered (92%)**, minimal returns/refunds  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Sections

### 1. Orders vs Sales
- Combo chart comparing **Sales Amount** and **Order Count** month-wise  
- Peak in **March**, followed by gradual decline till year-end  

### 2. Orders: Age vs Gender
- Bar chart showing:  
  - **Adults (Women: 34.59%)** lead overall orders  
  - Seniors and Teenagers show smaller contribution  

### 3. Sales: Top 5 States
- Horizontal bar chart for top-performing states:  
  - **Maharashtra** – ₹2.99M  
  - Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu  

### 4. Orders by Channels
- Pie chart showing share of orders by platforms:  
  - **Amazon (35%)**, **Myntra (23%)**, **Flipkart (22%)**, others include Meesho, Ajio, Nalli  

### 5. Sales: Men vs Women
- Donut chart indicating:  
  - **Women: 64%**  
  - **Men: 36%**  

### 6. Order Status
- Pie chart breakdown:  
  - **Delivered: 92%**  
  - **Cancelled: 3%**  
  - **Returned: 2%**  
  - **Refunded: 2%**

---

## 🕓 Filters/Controls

- **Month Filter**: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May  
- **Channel Filter**: Ajio, Amazon, Flipkart  
- **Category Filter**: Blouse, Bottom, Ethnic Dress  

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** – Dashboard creation & data modeling
